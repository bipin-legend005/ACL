OFFICIAL COMPETITION POLICY

🏏 RCL TOURNAMENT RULES

1. Locked Teams 🇮🇳

Each team represents one national or international team. The selection is locked for the tournament once play begins.

Organizer changes are allowed only before the first league result is recorded.

2. Powerplay ⚡

Powerplay: First 2 overs of every match.

3. Network Issue 📶

The organizer must manually confirm a genuine connectivity issue. The match becomes NO RESULT, both teams receive 1 point, and neither gets a win or loss.

4. No-Show 🚫

A confirmed no-show is a FORFEIT: the opponent receives 2 points and the absent team receives 0.

5. Quit / Surrender 🏳️

A confirmed surrender is a FORFEIT — QUIT: the opponent receives 2 points and the quitting team receives 0.

Points System

Win: 2 points. No Result / Draw: 1 point each. No-Show or Quit: opponent gets 2 points. Loss: 0 points.
